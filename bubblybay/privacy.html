Privacy Policy – Bubbly Bay: Ocean Match

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Español

This policy explains how the mobile game Bubbly Bay: Ocean Match (package com.bubblybaygames.oceanmatch), published on Google Play by Bubbly Bay Games (Eugenio Savini, Lugano, Switzerland), handles your information. Contact: eugeniosavini@gmail.com.

1. Data stored on your device

Game progress (levels, stars, coins, lives, settings, language) is saved locally on your device. You can delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

2. Optional sign-in and cloud save

If you choose to sign in with Google (or Facebook, when available), we receive your account ID, name, email address and profile picture from that provider, and we store your game progress in Google Firebase (Firestore, servers in Europe) so you can recover it on another device. Signing in is optional.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded videos and occasional interstitial ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, in line with Google's policies. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you will be asked for consent for personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Premium subscribers do not see forced ads.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases and the Premium subscription are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details; we only receive confirmation of the purchase to unlock it in the game.

5. Analytics, crash reports and reviews

We may use Google Firebase services to collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to improve the game. The in-game rating prompt uses Google Play In-App Review; we do not see who leaves a review.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play, and Facebook if you use Facebook sign-in), which act under their own privacy policies.

7. Retention and your rights

Cloud save data is kept while your account is active. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or delete your account, by emailing eugeniosavini@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Switzerland, the FDPIC).

8. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security and changes

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
